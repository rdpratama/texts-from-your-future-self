# Texts From Your Future Self

One funny text message from your future self, 153 characters, for [Texts From Your Future Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-texts-from-your-future-self-dd44595e) on make.fun:

> hey, it's you from 2041. this is my 47th text and you've left me on read since 2026. reply already — i'm literally you and even i can't get a hold of us.

The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

- **A real joke with a twist:** the future self has been texting for 15 years and getting left on read — by himself. The punchline lands on "even i can't get a hold of us."
- **Uses the medium itself:** read receipts, unanswered texts, the 47th attempt — it could only exist as a text message, which is exactly what the bounty asks for.
- **Specific voice and details:** 2041, 47th text, left on read since 2026 — concrete numbers that make it sound like one person's actual future self.
- **Fresh angle:** not the obvious "still broke" or "still tired" joke, and not the password / sourdough / houseplant families the field is full of.
- **Economical:** 153 characters, one breath, punchline arrives fast.
